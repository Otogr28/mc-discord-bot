**Changelog — June 24, 2026**

A batch of quality-of-life mods and companion upgrades landed this week. Here's what's new:

**New mods**
- **Falling Tree.** Break one log and the whole tree comes down at once, leaves and all. It's tuned to spare your builds: log pillars and beams with no leaves on top stay standing.
- **Tom's Simple Storage.** Link your chests with an Inventory Connector and a Storage Terminal to browse and search everything from one screen. Inventory Cables reach the chests further away.

**Companions**
- **Feed them a golden apple to heal.** Right-click your companion with a **golden apple** in hand and it eats it: **Regeneration II** (5s), **Absorption I** (2 min), and an instant **1-heart** heal. Costs one apple.
- **Respawn time is now a flat 1:30.** A fallen companion takes **90 seconds** to return, at every level. Respawn-reduction upgrades still shorten the wait.
- **Pick their combat stance.** Warriors and mages now have a **Combat** toggle in the Equip tab: **Passive** (never fights), **Defensive** (only defends), **Neutral** (helps what you attack), or **Hostile** (hunts nearby threats).
- **Summons wear your name.** A Summoner's minions now show your name in **yellow** above them, visible through walls, so they're easy to tell apart from wild mobs.
- **See your picto progress.** The Pictos tab now shows a **kills / needed** counter next to each equipped picto, so you know exactly how close a lumina is to unlocking.

Two of these are new mods (Falling Tree, Tom's Storage), so pull the latest modpack to get them.

See you at the Gates.
