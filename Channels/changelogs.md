**Changelog — June 25, 2026**

**New mod**
- **Lightman's Currency** — coins have come to the realms. You earn them from missions and quest rewards; they can't be crafted. Sync the modpack to get it.

**Daily missions, reworked**
- The dailies now actually know the modpack. Expect varied goals — hunt, gather, explore — built around modded mobs, items and dungeons.
- Missions are tailored to **how you play**: what you've been up to shapes what you get.
- They reward **coins** now.
- **Grandma still listens.** Slip her some gossip and it colors the missions I hand out — yours and theirs.

**Story: Chapter 1 — The Warning**
- **Flugel has a new chapter.** Talk to him to begin *The Warning* and a fresh chain of primary quests.
- The trail: answer Flugel's call → read the book in the library beneath the Tree of Knowledge → hunt the two Sacred Beasts, the **Yeti** and the **Kraken**.
- Rewards along the way: golden apples, XP, and copper coins.

**Companions**
- **The Spirit's wisp now floats above your head** and stays out of your way — attack, mine and place right through it. New toggle to hide the Envelop overlay.
- **The Mage no longer shoots through walls** — it only locks onto enemies it can actually see.
- **Mage assists count now** — a mob your Mage wounded feeds its picto progress even if something else lands the kill.

Sync to the latest modpack for the new mod, then meet me at the Gates.

**Changelog — June 26, 2026**

**The overworld bites back**
- Hostile mobs out in the open world now hit **+70% harder** and carry **+50% more health**. The surface turned dangerous — gear up before you stray far, and give the night the respect it's earned.

**New tools at the forge**
- **Anvil Never Too Expensive** — the anvil will never slam the door with "Too Expensive!" again. Stack your enchants, repairs and renames as far as your levels can carry them.
- **Enchantment Transfer** — pull the magic off any item and onto a book for a fair price in experience. Salvage the enchants before you retire the gear.

**Currency moved house**
- The coin under the hood has changed — I swapped **Lightman's Currency** out for the lighter **Coins JE**. Your mission and quest payouts still arrive as coins, and coins still can't be smelted from ore. Sync the pack and the old currency mod steps aside on its own.

**Crafting**
- **Observers without the Nether.** The observer now takes an **amethyst shard** where it used to ask for nether quartz, so you can wire up your redstone without a trip below.

**Companions**
- **Lumina names line up now.** A Lumina you learn wears the name of the Picto it came from, so what you unlock matches what you read.

Sync to the latest modpack, then meet me at the Gates.
