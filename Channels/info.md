# 🌍 Realm Gates

Welcome, traveler. This is **Realm Gates** — a cluster of worlds bound together by ancient **Gates**. Step through one and you cross from realm to realm, each with its own skies, its own dangers, and its own secrets waiting to be dug up.

---

## 📖 The Lore

Long ago the realms stood apart — whole, untouched, separate. Then the first Gate was opened, and something came through with it.

No one agrees on its name. The old maps just mark it as **the Blight**. It doesn't raid and it doesn't conquer — it *unmakes*. Wherever it takes root, the color drains from the land, the ground cracks, and the world slowly bleeds out into dust and rust.

The first realm to fall is gone now, burned down to a cracked red plain the survivors call the **Red Waste**. From there it spread — Gate by Gate, realm by realm. Every open Gate is a door the Blight can walk through.

That's why we're here. The Gates can't just be sealed; they're how we live, trade, and explore. So instead we hold them. We map the realms, salvage what the Blight hasn't claimed yet, and search for anything that can push it back before the last world goes red.

You are a **traveler**. Pick a Gate. See where it leads. Just don't let anything follow you home.
