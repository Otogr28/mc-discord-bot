**Changelog — June 24, 2026**

Tonight's update is about keeping your companion alive and standing by your side.

- **Feed your companion a golden apple to heal it.** Right-click your companion with a **golden apple** in hand and it eats it like you would: **Regeneration II** for 5 seconds, **Absorption I** for 2 minutes, and an instant **1-heart** heal. The apple is consumed.
- **Companion respawn time is now a flat 1:30.** A fallen companion takes **90 seconds** to return, at every level (it used to start at 60 seconds and shrink as the companion leveled up). Respawn-reduction upgrades still cut into that wait.

See you at the Gates.
